Over the past few years, I have used LLMs and AI in many, many projects, with varying degrees of self-discipline and care for the results. I've done very careful professional engineering work with AI assistance as part of my job, and I've produced some absolute vibe-coded slop for my personal amusement or as pure experimentation, and I've done everything in between.

I've learned, for many different situations, where to draw the line.

But that line can be fuzzy, and it can shift over time, and it can be difficult to explain. I think clear boundaries and absolute are the best policies, especially in this case, where my education and abilities, not to mention my professional credibility and my integrity, are at stake.

I will not use LLMs to generate any code or content or any other text within this repository, even if it might seem reasonably distanced from the core mission of ARENA, except when directed to do so by the content of the course itself.

For instance, that is why the CSS for this blog will remain rather cold and unpleasant. I'm not a frontend developer, I have little skill with visual aesthetics, and I have even less time 🙂

I expect to consult LLMs about the course content and the questions it raises for me numerous times - frankly, Claude and ChatGPT have been invaluable for my personal and professional development over the past few years - but I will never, at any time, direct them to act within this repository or copy and paste content from them into this repository or in any other way introduce any information, content, code, etc that is not my own creation.

I technically started ARENA yesterday (11/01/2026), but that was just the project setup[^1].

Today, I reviewed the planner and appreciated the full depth and scale of the ARENA program and how much I would have to work each day to complete it in a reasonable timeframe[^2].

And then I reviewed 0.0, or [Prerequisites](https://learn.arena.education/chapter0_fundamentals/00_prereqs/1-core-concepts-knowledge/).

Before going further, I think it's probably worth explaining a bit of my background, especially with regard to mathematics. I graduated high school with a cumulative 1.667 (D+) GPA[^3]. I unfortunately have what I refer to as "weapons-grade" AuDHD[^4], which went undiagnosed until I was 36 and untreated until I was 44. It was essentially impossible for me to focus or effectively prioritize work. I failed most of my classes throughout middle and high school, and I think I was graduated largely so they wouldn't have to figure out something else to do with me. This was not conducive to a meaningful mathematical education 🙃

From the time I was a child, I wanted to be a scientist and to work with computers. But I thought I was stupid, and so I ruled those possibilities out until I was well into adulthood.

The US Navy prized my ASVAB score over my high school transcript, and so I enlisted and entered into the nuclear power training pipeline. My service isn't terribly relevant except for that it's how I managed to go back to college and graduate, at 31, with a degree in Computer Science, and why I am now working as a contractor for the Department of Veterans Affairs, trying to improve access to care and benefits for other veterans.

But my mathematical skills never really developed. I struggled to pass Calculus, and I graduated having felt like I just scraped by.

In August of 2025, I started using [MathAcademy](https://www.mathacademy.com). I started with integrated math and am now roughly at the point where I was when I graduated college - linear algebra, differential and integral calculus, and probability and statistics.

So this is a long way of saying that I'm approaching the prerequisites for ARENA with some caution.

After some thought, I've decided to spend this week (11/02-11/06) focusing on some conceptual and programming prerequisites:
- Neural Networks: [3Blue1Brown chapters 1–4](https://www.youtube.com/playlist?list=PLZHQObOWTQDNU6R1_67000Dx_ZCJB-3pi)
- Python:
  - Einops: [Basics Tutorial](https://einops.rocks/1-einops-basics/) and some exercises
  - Einsum: [Einsum Is All You Need](https://rockt.github.io/2018/04/30/einsum) and some exercises
  - PyTorch: [What Is torch.nn Really?](https://pytorch.org/tutorials/beginner/nn_tutorial.html)

I've also added the rest of the material where I feel weak or underdeveloped (which is _a lot_) as work to do alongside ARENA where most appropriate.

First up, I have some videos to watch 🙂
